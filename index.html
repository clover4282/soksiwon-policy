"속시원한 이야기 운영" is a private command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel 속시원한 이야기 to manage that channel through YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What is accessed: the owner's own channel settings, the owner's own videos (to upload them), and audience retention reports for the owner's own videos.
  • How it is used: to upload videos and to record how many viewers keep watching at 3 and 30 seconds, so future videos can be improved.
  • Storage: the OAuth token and the retention numbers are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with, or shown to any third party. No data about other users or channels is collected.
  • Retention and deletion: retention numbers are kept as the owner's own notes. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, after which the local token is deleted.
  • Cookies: this tool does not use cookies or tracking.
